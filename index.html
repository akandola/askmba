The MBA in the Age of AI

  • AI is commoditizing execution across every business function. The tasks that used to define careers now take minutes.
  • The MBA trains what AI can't replace: judgment, the ability to set direction and make high-stakes decisions with incomplete information.
  • As AI pushes humans up the value chain toward higher-order strategic thinking, the MBA becomes more valuable — especially for operators from tech.
